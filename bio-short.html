Short biography

Oriana Bandiera · Sir Anthony Atkinson Chair in Economics, London School of Economics

Oriana Bandiera is the Sir Anthony Atkinson Chair in Economics at the London School of Economics. She studies how organisations and labour markets shape opportunity, work and economic development. Her research explores why some people remain trapped in poverty, how people move into better jobs, and how incentives and social relationships influence behaviour in firms and public services.

She is based in LSE’s Department of Economics and STICERD. She served as President of the European Economic Association in 2022 and Co-Editor of Econometrica from 2019 to 2023. Her honours include the Birgit Grodal Award (2026), the Yrjö Jahnsson Award, the Ester Boserup Prize and the Carlo Alberto Medal.
